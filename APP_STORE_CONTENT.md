## Description (170 characters max for short, unlimited for full)

**Short Description (170 chars):**

**Full Description:**

Transform your classroom into an engaging learning adventure with Class Pets!

**Features:**

🎮 **Virtual Pets for Students**
- Students collect and level up their own virtual pets
- Pets grow and evolve as students earn points
- Each student gets a unique classroom companion

⭐ **Gamification Made Simple**
- Award points for homework, participation, and good behavior
- Students spend points at the class store
- Track progress with visual level-up system

👥 **Smart Group Management**
- Create groups manually or randomly assign
- Track group leaderboards
- Foster teamwork and collaboration

🎲 **Fair Random Selection**
- Weighted random picker considers recent picks
- Everyone gets a fair chance to participate
- Perfect for classroom activities

📊 **Leaderboards**
- Individual and group rankings
- See who's leading in real-time
- Celebrate achievements

**Perfect for:**
- Elementary and middle school teachers
- Classroom management and student engagement
- Making learning fun and interactive

**Privacy:**
We take privacy seriously. All data is stored securely and never shared.
